# Scroll-craft editorial trial

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. User approved a scroll-craft trial, with all news readable, the production site untouched, and no new paid service.

## Eight design decisions

1. Vibe: editorial, calm, dimensional, useful. Existing navy, paper and dark-mode palette retained.
2. Journey: title page, featured news and planning implication, issue highlights and fixtures, digital agenda, complete articles and sources, closing notes and archive.
3. Energy: restrained opening, one high-contrast editorial spread, then a quiet reading surface.
4. Feeling curve: orientation through date and full count; curiosity through a real lead story; clarity when its planning implication is paired with the story; control through the complete indexed feed; confidence through sources and review notes; resolve through saved reading and previous issues.
5. Signature: the reading companion follows the article currently in view and shows its actual planning action. A reader can copy that action with the article link. It uses live bulletin data, not invented advice.
6. Range: editorial. Established colors and two typefaces are brand inputs.
7. Separate editorial chapters, no continuous world, scroll hijacking or mandatory cinematic introduction.
8. Assets: existing bulletin photographs, captions and source links. No generated imagery or paid video API.

## Grammar and peak

Chaptered editorial: title page, asymmetric lead spread, margin navigation, stable body copy, colophon ending. The other grammars lose because the site is long-form research rather than a film, demo, physical journey, manifesto, product range, two-sided argument or fast event campaign.

Peak: “As I read a story, its practical action follows beside it.” The full article reading region carries the greatest span. The introduction is shorter and optional via a direct link to all articles. No authored empty scroll.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Static type and real count on entry | Immediate orientation |
| Lead spread | Flow act with restrained image parallax | Visual depth, stable text |
| Issue context | One-time entrance | Settle the highlights and sports panel |
| Full feed | Reveal on chapter rules; bespoke reading companion | Show location and next action without hiding copy |
| Close | Static colophon | Keep notes and archive reachable |

## Fingerprint

First scroll-craft build in this project. Compared with the prior Hyperframes trial: grammar changes from video-led feed to chaptered editorial, navigation adds a story index, hero becomes a title page, sequence becomes a lead spread and full feed, close retains original content, signature becomes the action companion. Five of six dimensions differ. Existing content requirements override any marketing-site advice to remove sections.

## Boundaries

Only deneme/ is writable. original/ remains byte-for-byte unchanged. Vendor engine remains unmodified with MIT notice. JS-disabled animation layer must never hide bulletin text; existing bulletin app still needs JavaScript to load its JSON. Reduced motion and the visible motion toggle remove decorative displacement. Mobile uses stacked text and imagery with no sticky action panel. No telemetry added.
